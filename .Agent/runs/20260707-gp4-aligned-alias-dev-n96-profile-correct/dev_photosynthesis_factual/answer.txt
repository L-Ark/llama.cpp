Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide into glucose (a sugar) and oxygen. It takes place mainly in the leaves of plants, using chlorophyll to capture light energy. The basic equation is: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ In short: plants use sunlight to make food and release oxygen.
